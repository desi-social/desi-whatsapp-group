# desi-whatsapp-group
Looking to connect with fellow desis in your city? Join our local [**Desi WhatsApp Group**](https://desiout.com/india) and become part of a friendly community of Indians, Pakistanis, Bangladeshis, Nepalis, and Sri Lankans living nearby. Whether you want to make new friends, find local events, discuss cricket and entertainment, get job or housing updates, or simply stay connected to your culture, this group is the perfect place to meet people around you. Students, families, and working professionals are all welcome. Come be part of a growing desi community near you!
